\section{Remarks}
\label{sec.further}

\paragraph{Rate of the certified height}

Let the client's chain have height $gr$ at round $r$, with
$\frac{t}{n-t}f\le g\le\frac{n}{n-t}f$. In the limiting form of
\Cref{cor.growth}, the certified height is
$\pref(r)=\frac{n}{n-t}\left(g-\frac{t}{n-t}f\right)r$. Its rate is at most
$g$, with equality only for $g=\frac{n}{n-t}f$. For the honest chain, $g=f$ and the certified height lags
the chain by $\frac{t^2}{(n-t)^2}fr$ blocks. The lag is necessary up to lower
order terms. By \Cref{thm.attack}, $\pipriv$ with $r_0=(1-\frac{t}{n-t})r$
and $s=\frac{t}{n-t}r$ produces at round $r$ a chain of height about $fr$
whose last $\frac{t^2}{(n-t)^2}fr$ blocks are on no honest chain, and a
client that knows only $r$ and $\len{\ch}$ cannot tell the two chains apart.

\paragraph{Open questions}

The additive terms of \Cref{thm.prefix,cor.round} are of order $\lambda f$,
the order of the common prefix parameter of~\cite{gkl24}. We do not claim
that they are optimal. The analysis is for the synchronous network
of~\cite{gkl24}. In the bounded delay model of~\cite{gkl24,pss16} the rate
$f$ is replaced by a rate that depends on the delay $\Delta$, and we expect
$t/(n-2t)$ to be replaced by the corresponding ratio of rates. The analysis
assumes a static population and a fixed target $T$. Variable difficulty
changes the relation between height and round on which \Cref{cor.round}
rests.
