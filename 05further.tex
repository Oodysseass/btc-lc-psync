\section{Remarks}
\label{sec.further}

\paragraph{Threshold at one third}

The coefficient $t/(n-2t)$ equals $1$ at $t=n/3$. Below one third it is
smaller than $1$, and pruning the length deficit $\len{\ch'}-\len{\ch}$ is
sound but wasteful. Between one third and one half it exceeds $1$, and pruning
the length deficit is unsound. The backbone protocol has common prefix, chain
quality and chain growth throughout that range. The reason is an
exchange rate. A private branch of $d$ blocks costs the adversary $d$ blocks. It costs the
honest validators only $\frac{n-2t}{t}d$ blocks of length advantage, because
they produce blocks at rate $f$ while the adversary produces them at rate
$\frac{t}{n-t}f$.

\paragraph{Admissible schedules}

In the limit, the prefix certified by \Cref{cor.round} has height
$\pref(r)=\frac{n}{n-t}\left(\hgt(r)-\frac{t}{n-t}fr\right)$. This
is non-decreasing in $r$ exactly when
$\hgt(r_2)-\hgt(r_1)\ge\frac{t}{n-t}f(r_2-r_1)$ for all $r_1<r_2$, that is,
when the schedule advances at average rate at least $\frac{t}{n-t}f$. A client
whose chain stalls loses guarantee, because the adversary gains time to mine a
private branch of that height. A schedule advancing at the guaranteed honest
rate $(1-\epsilon)f$ gives
\begin{align*}
    \pref(r_2)-\pref(r_1) &= \frac{n}{n-t}\left((1-\epsilon)-\frac{t}{n-t}\right)f\,(r_2-r_1)\\
    &< (1-\epsilon)f\,(r_2-r_1),
\end{align*}
so the certified prefix advances strictly more slowly than the honest chain.
At the ends of the band, a schedule with $\hgt(r)\ge\maxr fr$ is not
realizable at round $r$ by \Cref{lem.length}, and a schedule with
$\hgt(r)\le\frac{t}{n-t}fr$ carries no guarantee by \Cref{cor.round}.

\paragraph{Use by a client}

A client holding several chains applies \Cref{cor.prune} to the longest one,
because the pruning depth of \Cref{cor.round} decreases with $\len{\ch}$. The
bound is a deterministic consequence of typicality and holds at once for every
chain of $\bt_r$, so no union bound over chains is needed. \Cref{cor.prune} is
the statement a client uses. After pruning $\prn(r)$ blocks, what remains is a
prefix of the chain of every honest validator, at round $r$ and at every later
round.

\paragraph{Open ends}

Three questions are open. First, we do not claim that the additive terms of
\Cref{thm.prefix,cor.round} are optimal. They are of order $\lambda f$, the
order of the common prefix parameter of~\cite{gkl24}, and $\pipriv$ says
nothing about them. Second, the analysis is for the
synchronous validator network of~\cite{gkl24}. In the bounded delay model of~\cite{gkl24,pss16} the rate $f$ is replaced by
a rate that accounts for the delay $\Delta$. We expect $t/(n-2t)$ to be
replaced by the corresponding ratio of rates, but we have not checked this. Third, the analysis assumes a
static population and a fixed target $T$. Variable difficulty changes the
relation between height and round on which \Cref{cor.round} rests.
