\caption{The network model. The validators are on Earth, and they are
synchronous with one another: a message from one of them reaches all the others
within a bounded delay that everybody knows. The client is on $\alpha$ Centauri,
and its communication with earth is asynchronous: a message may take any amount
of time to arrive, or it may never arrive, and the client cannot tell which.
What the client does have is a clock, so it knows the current round, and the
parameters of the protocol, which are public.}\label{fig.model}
