\caption{The network model. The validators are on earth, and they are
synchronous with one another: a message from one of them reaches all the others
within a bounded delay $\Delta$ that everybody knows. One of them, in red, is
adversarial. The client is on its way to $\alpha$ Centauri, and its
communication with earth is asynchronous: a chain sent to it may take any amount of time to
arrive, or may never arrive, and the client cannot tell which. What the client
does have is a clock, so it knows the current round $r$, and the parameters of
the protocol, which are public.}\label{fig.model}
