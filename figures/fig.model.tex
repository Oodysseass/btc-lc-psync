\caption{The network model. The validators are on Earth, and they are
synchronous with one another: a message from one of them reaches all the
others within a bounded delay that everybody knows. The whole analysis of
bitcoin rests on this. The client is on $\alpha$ Centauri, and its link to
Earth is asynchronous: a message may take any amount of time to arrive, or it
may never arrive, and the client is never told which. What the client does have
is a clock, so it knows the current round, and it knows the parameters of the
protocol, which are public. It knows nothing else.}\label{fig.model}
